development dependency is pinned to the same release. Upstream's
[release notes](https://github.com/earendil-works/pi/releases/tag/v0.99.1)
and [changelog](https://github.com/earendil-works/pi/blob/v0.99.1/packages/coding-agent/CHANGELOG.md)

MCP/codemode and input dispositions. This check does not claim new OAR
capabilities for those additions.
