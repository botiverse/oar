# Runtime version check: 2026-11-22

The scheduled candidate run passed 39 of 42 jobs. All three failures were
the same two Antigravity skill assertions on Linux, macOS and Windows.
Separately, two Windows platform shards could install their runtimes but
could not launch five test commands. The version freeze, later release
inventory and all 13 failed jobs are in the [selected evidence](2026-11-22.json).
[Candidate run](https://github.com/botiverse/oar/actions/runs/44993977193),
[platform run](https://github.com/botiverse/oar/actions/runs/44980528308).

at OAR `f0e41bf8`. The follow-up queried 15 release entries at 02:10 UTC
on November 23, at `3edc3cbf`. Factory 1.17.2 was published at 03:08 UTC
on November 22, before the freeze. Qwen 0.28.4 was published at 12:28 UTC,
after the freeze and before the reporting cutoff. Local tests used Linux
